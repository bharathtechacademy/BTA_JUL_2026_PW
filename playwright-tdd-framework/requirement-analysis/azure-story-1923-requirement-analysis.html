⚠ Risk Assessment & Mitigation Strategy

5 Active Vectors
Plaintext Password Leakage & Toggle Bug High Risk
Impact: If the eye toggle fails to reset input type back to "password" or leaks into browser history/console logs, user credentials may be compromised.
QA Mitigation Strategy: Verify input type strictly toggles between "password" and "text"; write Playwright assertion ensuring type="password" on form submit.
Brute Force & Credential Stuffing Vulnerability High Risk
Impact: Automated brute-force attacks could compromise accounts without rate-limiting, CAPTCHA, or account lockout thresholds.
QA Mitigation Strategy: Implement and verify account lockout or rate-limiting (HTTP 429) after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
Social OAuth Redirect Hijacking & Open Redirects Medium Risk
Impact: Misconfigured redirect_uri parameters can allow token leakage or open redirection to phishing domains.
QA Mitigation Strategy: Validate exact redirect_uri whitelist on OAuth provider responses and test user consent cancellation flows.
Cookie Consent Withdrawal Impact on Authentication Medium Risk
Impact: If a user withdraws cookie consent, necessary authentication cookies might inadvertently be blocked or non-essential tracking cookies persisted.
QA Mitigation Strategy: Validate strict categorization: strictly necessary cookies remain operational; marketing/analytics cookies cleared immediately upon withdrawal.
Third-Party Script Latency (Chatbot / Social SDKs) Low Risk
Impact: If external CDNs experience network latency, login form could suffer layout shift or delayed interactivity.
QA Mitigation Strategy: Ensure async/defer script loading with non-blocking fallback UI in Playwright simulated network failure tests.

ℹ Working Assumptions & Test Constraints

5 Architecture Baselines
Technical Architecture & Stack
The Creatio CRM login interface is implemented as a secure Single Page Application (SPA) communicating over HTTPS with authentication microservices returning signed JWT bearer tokens.
Session & Cookie Management
Successful authentication sets secure, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookies. Unauthenticated requests are strictly redirected to the login gateway.
Third-Party Social OAuth Integrations
OAuth 2.0 / OIDC integrations for Google, LinkedIn, and Facebook utilize preconfigured sandbox client IDs and redirect URIs in staging test environments.
Cookie Consent & Chatbot SDKs
Cookie consent widget and Creatio FAQ chatbot are loaded as asynchronous SDK scripts without degrading form rendering performance or blocking user interaction.
Pre-seeded Test Accounts
QA database maintains pre-seeded accounts representing active users, locked accounts, unverified registrations, and federated social login personas.
