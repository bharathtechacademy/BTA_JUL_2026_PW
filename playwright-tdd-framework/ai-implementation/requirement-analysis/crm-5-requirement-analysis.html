⚠️ QA Risk Assessment & Mitigation Strategies
5 Assessed Risks
Plaintext Password Exposure in DOM / Toggle Bug High Risk

If eye toggle fails to reset type back to "password" or leaks into logs, user credentials may be compromised.

QA Mitigation: Verify input type strictly toggles between "password" and "text"; ensure automated scripts verify type="password" on form submit.
Brute Force & Credential Stuffing High Risk

Attacker automated attacks could compromise accounts without rate-limiting or CAPTCHA.

QA Mitigation: Implement and test account lockout or rate-limiting (HTTP 429) after 5 consecutive failed attempts.
Social Login Redirect Hijacking / OAuth Misconfiguration Medium Risk

Misconfigured redirect_uri can allow token leakage or open redirection vulnerabilities.

QA Mitigation: Validate exact redirect_uri whitelist on OAuth provider responses and test cancellation flows.
Cookie Consent Withdrawal Impact Medium Risk

If user withdraws cookie consent, necessary auth cookies might inadvertently be blocked or non-essential cookies persisted.

QA Mitigation: Validate strict categorization: strictly necessary cookies remain operational; marketing/analytics cookies cleared immediately on withdrawal.
Slow Loading of Third-Party Scripts (Chatbot/Social) Low Risk

If external CDN fails, login form might experience layout shift or delayed interactivity.

QA Mitigation: Ensure async/defer script loading with fallback UI in Playwright network failure tests.
💡 Key Assumptions & Test Constraints
5 Assumptions
Technical & Architecture

Login form is built as a Single Page Application (SPA) communicating over HTTPS with authentication REST APIs returning standard JWT bearer tokens.

Session & State Management

Successful authentication sets secure, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookies. Unauthenticated requests redirect back to login.

Third-Party Social Auth

OAuth 2.0 / OIDC integrations for Google, LinkedIn, and Facebook utilize sandbox/test client IDs in staging environments.

Cookie & Chatbot Widgets

Cookie consent manager and Creatio chatbot are loaded as asynchronous third-party or internal SDK scripts without blocking form interaction.

User Accounts

Test accounts with active, inactive, locked, and unverified states are seeded in the QA database.
